Orbit

Day planner for iOS and Android

Plan less. Focus more.

Orbit lays out your day as time blocks on a timeline, runs a focus timer with built-in lo‑fi and singing-bowl sounds, and shows an almanac page for each day. Your data is stored on your phone.

  • DayDrag blocks onto the timeline, then move or resize them.
  • FocusA countdown ring with optional background sound, generated on the phone.
  • AlmanacMoon phase and tarot in English; 宜忌, solar terms and a daily 签 in Chinese.
  • PrivateWorks without an account. No server, no tracking.
