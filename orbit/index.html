Orbit

Day planner · 日晷

Plan less. Focus more.

Orbit lays out your day as time blocks on a timeline, runs a focus timer, and keeps an almanac page for each day. You can use it as a guest. An account is optional. On a Chinese home screen the app is named 日晷; in English it is Orbit.

What Orbit does

  • DayAdd a block, drag it onto the day, move it, resize it, or drop it back in the tray. A week strip sits above the timeline.
  • FocusA countdown ring timed by the clock, so it stays correct in the background. Optional lo‑fi and singing-bowl sound, generated on the device. You can add 10 minutes.
  • AlmanacA page for each day. In Chinese: 宜 / 忌, the twelve double-hours, solar terms, the moon and a daily 签. In English: the moon, a sun sign, a tarot card, a lucky colour and number. It is traditional lore, for culture and curiosity, not advice.
  • RemindersLocal notifications before a block starts and when a focus session ends. The switch starts on, and you can turn it off. There is no push server.
  • CalendarOff until you turn on Add to Calendar. Blocks are written into a calendar named “Orbit”, one way. On iPhone it is placed with iCloud when that is possible. On Android it stays on the device.
  • WidgetsOn iPhone, Home Screen widgets can show the coming week, the focus timer and the almanac.
  • Guest or accountContinue as guest with no account. Or sign in with Apple (iPhone and the web, not Android), Google, or an email code.
  • Encrypted syncOff unless an Apple or email account turns it on. Plans are encrypted on the device. The server stores ciphertext only. Google sign-in does not turn sync on.

Languages: English, 简体中文 and 繁體中文. Export writes a JSON backup you keep; import replaces the plans on that device. Orbit does not include analytics or advertising.

Do I need an account?

No. Choose Continue as guest on the welcome screen and the planner works on this device. Signing in with Apple or email lets you turn on encrypted sync. Signing in with Google puts your name, email and photo on this device; it does not create an Orbit server account and does not turn sync on. The first time you sign in, guest plans on this device are copied to the account if that account is still empty.

How do I add a block?

Tap New block, give it a name, a type and a length, then drag it from the tray onto the day. Hold a block to move it. Tap it for Focus, done, edit and delete.

Widgets and Calendar

On iPhone, long-press the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Orbit (or 日晷 if the phone is in Chinese). To see blocks in Calendar, turn on Me → Add to Calendar. Edits you make in Calendar are not copied back into Orbit.

Delete your account

In the app: Me → Delete account and data, then tap again to confirm. Guest mode has no account to delete. If you cannot open the app, see how to delete your account, including the email request to support@mossyware.com.

Orbit（中文主屏幕名称：日晷）是一款日程应用：时间块、专注计时和每日黄历。可以不注册，以游客身份使用。标语：少则得，专则明。

必须注册账号吗？

不需要。在欢迎页选择「以游客身份继续」。用 Apple 或邮箱登录后，可以开启加密同步。Google 登录只把姓名、邮箱和头像留在这台设备上，不会建立 Orbit 服务器账号，也不会开启同步。

怎么添加任务块？

点「新建任务块」，填写名称、类型和时长，再从托盘拖到当天的时间线上。按住可以移动，点一下可以专注、完成、编辑或删除。

小组件、日历和删除账号

在 iPhone 主屏幕长按，添加小组件，搜索 Orbit 或日晷。想在系统日历里看到任务块，打开「我的 → 同步到系统日历」。删除账号：我的 →「删除账号和数据」，再点一次确认。打不开 App 时，请看删除步骤。

支援 · 繁體中文

Orbit（中文主畫面名稱：日晷）是一款行程應用：時間塊、專注計時和每日黃曆。可以不註冊，以遊客身份使用。標語：少則得，專則明。

寄信到 support@mossyware.com，通常兩個工作天內回覆。發行者：Zheng Li（MossyWare）。

隱私政策：https://mossyware.com/orbit/privacy。刪除帳號：https://mossyware.com/orbit/privacy#delete-account。

一定要註冊帳號嗎？

不需要。在歡迎頁選擇「以遊客身份繼續」。用 Apple 或電子郵件登入後，可以開啟加密同步。Google 登入只把姓名、電子郵件和頭像留在這台裝置上，不會建立 Orbit 伺服器帳號，也不會開啟同步。

怎麼加入任務區塊？

點「新建任務區塊」，填寫名稱、類型和時長，再從托盤拖到當天的時間線上。按住可以移動，點一下可以專注、完成、編輯或刪除。

小工具、日曆和刪除帳號

在 iPhone 主畫面長按，加入小工具，搜尋 Orbit 或日晷。想在系統日曆裡看到任務區塊，打開「我的 → 同步到系統日曆」。刪除帳號：我的 →「刪除帳號和資料」，再點一次確認。打不開 App 時，請看刪除步驟。
