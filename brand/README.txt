MossyWare logo kit
==================

Seta mark with Geist Medium wordmark. Production-ready exports.

Colour
------
  Near-black    #1A1A17
  Moss          #4A5D3A
  Moss (on dark)#7A9163   slightly lifted so the capsule stays visible on #1A1A17
  Off-white     #F4F3EE   app icon light plate

Wordmark
--------
  'MossyWare' only (mixed case, no slogan).
  Outlines derived from Geist Medium (weight 500), SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  See LICENSE-OFL-Geist.txt. Tracking -0.008em, with optical kerning on Mo, yW, Wa, ar.

Lockups
-------
  Horizontal: mark slightly taller than cap height (1.10×); ring / moss-pad
  baseline aligns with the word baseline; gap is three ring-strokes.
  Stacked: mark centred above the name, 1.18× cap height, gap of 2.25 ring-strokes.

Variants
--------
  color     black mark, moss capsule
  mono      all #1A1A17
  reversed  white artwork (transparent SVG/PNG) with a light-moss capsule
            Place reversed files on a dark surface. App icons already include a plate.

Small mark
----------
  A one-ring simplification for 16–32px (favicons). Prefer mark-small-* or the
  files in icon/ at those sizes. The full mark is for 48px and up.

Files
-----
  svg/     mark, mark-small, wordmark, horizontal, stacked, app-icon (all variants)
  png/     mark 512 / 1024; lockups and wordmark at 1200 and 2400 wide (transparent)
  icon/    app-icon-light-1024, app-icon-dark-1024, favicon.ico (16/32/48),
           favicon-16/32/48.png, apple-touch-icon.png (180, light) and -dark
  board.png  presentation sheet

The SVGs are outlined paths and do not need the font installed.
